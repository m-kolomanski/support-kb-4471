The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants all write the day first, so in the app, by email and through booking-v2 forms, 7 April comes out as 4 July. Fixed on 5 October. The desk view counts 379 reports here: the 374 above plus the 5 one-day moves it files under parser by tenant.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. Its date field shows MM/DD/YYYY, people follow it, and the form then reads their date day first. The parser fix does not touch this. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here. Intake tags these by the reporter’s browser, so its hint tag and this group disagree; this group follows the tenant (causes.ts).

one-day-move: the date moves by one day, not month for day. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” A client in Lisbon and one in Toronto got different days for one meeting; an invite arrived before the booking was made. That is a time zone or midnight conversion bug, not a parser one, and the parser fix leaves it in place. Still open. The desk noted these reports (desk-notes.json) but CauseTable still counts them under parser by tenant, so they vanish from view.
